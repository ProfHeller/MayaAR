Tikal, Petén, Guatemala · Early Classic

Stela 31

9.0.10.0.0 · dedicated 445 CE

Carved limestone monument of the Tikal king Siyaj Chan K'awiil II. The front shows the ruler in full regalia; the two sides show his father, Yax Nuun Ahiin, dressed as a Teotihuacan-style warrior. The back carries one of the longest Early Classic hieroglyphic texts at Tikal, a dynastic history that includes the arrival of Sihyaj K'ahk' in 378 CE.
